# Limitations

- The data describe a Portuguese bank's campaigns from 2008–2010. Validity in another population, institution, channel, product, regulation, or period is unresolved.
- “Unknown” categorical values are information gaps even though the repository labels the dataset as having no missing values.
- Exact timestamps, customer-level stable identifiers, consent, vulnerability, cost, harm, and protected-attribute governance are unavailable.
- Ordered holdout evaluation is retrospective and does not establish prospective performance.
- The system does not establish causal effects of contact or intervention.
- The AUC interval covers limited sampling uncertainty only.
- Threshold trade-offs use illustrative costs only; no threshold is economically, ethically, or operationally authorised.
- No fairness claim or certification is made.
- Calibration diagnostics are bin-dependent and reveal material error in high-score bands; no prospective recalibration has been completed.
- Target-proxy detection is a conservative heuristic and cannot identify every semantic or temporal leakage path.
- No production security, monitoring, incident response, MLOps, or enterprise integration is claimed.
- The system has no authority or mechanism to contact, deny, price, rank, or otherwise act upon a person.
- V1 services are deterministic workflow modules; they are not evidence of general autonomous reasoning.
