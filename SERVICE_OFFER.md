# Pre-Action Model Evidence Review
Author: Shofi Ahmed Uddin

## Buyer problem
Expose leakage, population shift and unreliable probabilities before predictive scores shape real decisions.

## Pilot contract
- Buyer: Analytics consultancies, CRM teams and AI delivery partners.
- Scope: One tabular binary-classification model, one intended use, and one agreed de-identified evaluation extract.
- Outputs: A leakage and validation review, calibration and threshold consequences, reproducible scripts, and an evidence-qualified decision brief.
- Inputs: supplied answers/data and reference documents; intended use; named accountable owner; agreed access route.
- Acceptance: every in-scope item is linked to evidence and a finding; every computed figure has a rerun route; missing inputs and unresolved judgments are explicit.
- Delivery: agree after a sample inspection. Scope or data changes require a revised agreement.
- Fee: fixed quotation after scoping; no automatic subscription.
- Exclusions: certification, legal opinion, production deployment, unrestricted security testing, guaranteed commercial outcomes.

## Evidence boundary
The client retains action authority. Historical ranking performance does not establish present-day calibration, consent, fairness or deployment readiness.
