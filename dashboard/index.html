Evidence-qualified conclusion

After excluding post-contact duration, the logistic model shows bounded retrospective ranking value on the later ordered holdout (ROC AUC 0.748; average precision 0.527) and improves Brier score over the training-prior baseline (0.191 versus 0.273). The large target-prevalence shift and historical source prevent a deployment or causal claim. Analysis may inform human review only; it does not authorise contact or another action.

Evidence chain

  1. EVIDENCEsource and hashes verified
  2. ASSUMPTIONSpre-contact moment fixed; post-contact duration excluded
  3. METHODinterpretable logistic regression plus prior baseline
  4. VALIDATIONordered 80/20 holdout and imbalance-aware metrics
  5. UNCERTAINTYapproximate AUC interval plus shift warning
  6. HARDENINGcalibration, threshold sensitivity and subgroup stability diagnostics
  7. AUTHORITYhuman approval required

Limitations visible at decision time

  • Historical Portuguese bank-campaign data from 2008-2010 may not transport to another institution, population, channel, or period.
  • The dataset documents campaign outcomes, not permission, vulnerability, fairness, cost, or customer harm.
  • Predicted probability is not authority to contact, deny, price, or otherwise act on a person.
  • The ordered holdout is stronger than a random split for this source but is not a prospective deployment trial.
  • Illustrative false-positive/false-negative costs are sensitivity inputs, not an operational threshold recommendation.
  • Subgroup diagnostics are descriptive stability checks and do not establish fairness or legal compliance.
