Pre-Action Model Evidence Review

Expose leakage, population shift and unreliable probabilities before predictive scores shape real decisions.

One bounded engagement

One tabular binary-classification model, one intended use, and one agreed de-identified evaluation extract.

Deliverables

A leakage and validation review, calibration and threshold consequences, reproducible scripts, and an evidence-qualified decision brief.

Acceptance

Every in-scope item has an evidence link, a finding and a clear statement of what remains unresolved. Computed figures have a reproducible check.

What to bring

A small sample, your intended use, the relevant source material or scores, and a named accountable owner. Access and handling are agreed before confidential material is shared.

Timing and fee

Fixed scope, fee and delivery date agreed after inspecting a sample. No subscription required.

Sample deliverable

A usable ranking can coexist with a 24.5 percentage-point outcome-rate shift. The review asks what that changes before scores trigger contact.
