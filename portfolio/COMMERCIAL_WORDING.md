## LinkedIn project description

Most Data Science demonstrations end when a model produces a score. A2 asks the harder question: when is that score admissible for action?

On a pinned 41,188-record public dataset, A2 detected a post-contact leakage feature, preserved temporal ordering, exposed severe population shift and high-band miscalibration, and withheld execution authority. The result is a reproducible evidence chain from source provenance to human review—not an automated decision engine.

## Client proposal paragraph

I propose a bounded Proof-before-Programme engagement around one decision workflow. I will reconstruct the decision moment, verify source and feature admissibility, model and validate the analytical question, expose uncertainty and error consequences, define human authority and safe-stop conditions, and deliver a replayable evidence record. A2 provides a working reference implementation of this method; production integration, regulatory conclusions and external execution would remain separately governed stages.

## UAE/Dubai version

For organisations accelerating Agentic AI and predictive automation, the immediate assurance gap is often not capability but admissibility: whether the current evidence, permissions and operating conditions justify execution. A2 demonstrates a practical control layer that preserves innovation while making leakage, uncertainty, authority and refusal visible before action. The initial engagement is deliberately bounded to one high-value workflow and produces evidence suitable for technical, risk and executive review.
